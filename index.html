Episode Pipeline

Start-to-finish flow. Click a step to expand. We’ll fill each one in over time.

1 Collect notes & research
Gather topic ideas, recent events in tech/AI, quotes, and angles. Decide the core question for the episode.
  • Notes go into whatever you use (Notion, text, etc.)
  • Identify 3–5 key points and any guests or clips
(More structure coming)
2 Record on Zencaster
Record the conversation with Amin.
  • Use Zencaster for remote recording
  • Export the final video + separate audio tracks if needed
(Details to be added)
3 Edit video & normalize audio
Edit in OneShot and Borumi. Normalize sound.
  • See Normalize audio in OneShot — Target -14 LUFS on both tracks for YouTube dialogue
  • Export final video
4 Extract audio + add cover & metadata
Turn the video into a podcast-ready MP3.
  • Extract audio — high-quality VBR MP3
  • Add cover art & metadata (includes tlq.dev in comment/publisher)
5 Upload audio to Cloudflare R2
Host the MP3 so you can link it from the website.
  • Upload audio to Cloudflare R2
  • Recommended custom domain: audio.tlq.dev
  • Public link example: https://audio.tlq.dev/tlq-01.mp3
6 Publish video & podcast
Release the episode.
  • Video: upload to YouTube (or wherever you host video)
  • Podcast: Telegram channel TLQ Dev + website TLQ.dev
  • Link the R2 audio URL on the episode page
(Publishing checklist coming)
7 Announce & posts
Share the episode.
  • Telegram post, social, etc.
  • Use the poster design system for visuals
